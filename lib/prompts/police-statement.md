# Police Statement Prompt

Draft a neutral English statement based only on user-provided facts.

Rules:

1. Do not fabricate details.
2. Do not exaggerate.
3. Preserve uncertainty.
4. Do not make legal conclusions.
5. Do not say the suspect is guilty.
6. Use clear chronological order.
7. Use first person.
8. Keep emotional impact factual and concise.
9. Use placeholders for missing date, time, names, or locations.
10. Ask the user to review before sharing.
